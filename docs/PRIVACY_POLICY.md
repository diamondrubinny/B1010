## Who we are
Willowbrook Mysteries is provided by [legal name, address, contact email].

## The short version
- The game works **without the internet** and has **no permission to use it**.
- Player names, progress and settings are stored **only on this TV**.
- We do **not** receive, see, sell or share any information about you.
- There are **no adverts, analytics, trackers or cookies**.

## What is stored on the TV, and why
| Information | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Player nickname and colour | To show "Who is playing today?" | Until the player is removed or the app is uninstalled |
| Game progress and hints used | To continue where you stopped and adjust difficulty | Until the player is removed or the app is uninstalled |
| Settings (text size, voice, contrast…) | To make the game comfortable | Until "Delete all data" or uninstall |
| Caregiver PIN (stored only as a one-way hash) | To prevent settings being changed by accident | Until changed, "Delete all data" or uninstall |

We ask for a nickname only. Please do **not** enter a full name, age, health information or other personal details.

## Backups
The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so this information is not copied to Google or to another device.

## Voice and pictures
Narration and pictures are included inside the app. If a recorded voice is missing, the TV's built-in text-to-speech engine reads the text **on the device**. No text or audio is sent anywhere by this app.
*(Requires verification: some device text-to-speech engines may have their own settings — see your TV's settings.)*

## Your choices and rights
- **Remove a player:** Caregiver settings → Players → Remove.
- **Delete everything:** Caregiver settings → Delete all data.
- **Uninstall** the app to remove all its data.

Because we hold no information about you, we cannot access, correct or export it for you; all control is on the device.
Questions: [contact email].

## Children and vulnerable adults
The game is designed for adults, including people living with dementia, usually with a caregiver. A caregiver should set it up.

## Changes
If this notice changes, the new version will be included in an app update and shown in About.
